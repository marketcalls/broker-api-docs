# Market Information

Access Upstox API market information endpoints for exchange status, trading hours, market holidays, and session timing details across all market segments.

## Endpoints

### Market Holidays

Retrieve the market holiday list for the current year. Plan your trading schedule around NSE, BSE, and MCX exchange closures ahead.

### Market Timings

Get market timing schedules for each exchange on a specific date. Get session open and close times for all trading segments.

### Exchange Status

Check the current market status for specific exchanges. Determine whether NSE, BSE, or MCX markets are open, closed, or in pre-open conditions.
